Every conductor in the vehicle, bundled, taped, routed and terminated as one assembly. On a modern car it can run to a couple of kilometres of wire and be among the heaviest single components after the powertrain.

## What it does

It carries power to every load and signals between every module. Wire gauge is chosen per circuit from current and length, because voltage drop matters — a long thin cable to a headlight makes a dim headlight.

Routing is engineering, not tidiness. The harness is kept away from heat sources, clipped at intervals so it cannot vibrate against structure, passed through rubber grommets wherever it crosses a panel, and given drip loops so water running along a cable falls off before reaching a connector.

Sensitive signal wires — the <Part id="electrical.can_bus" />, sensor returns — are twisted or shielded and routed away from high-current cables, because current in one wire induces noise in a neighbour.

## How it fails

Chafing where a clip has broken, corroded connector pins, and rodent damage. Faults are usually intermittent and vibration-dependent.

## How it is serviced

Not serviced. Repaired at the individual circuit.
