A pleated filter in the fresh-air path, usually behind the glovebox. It is the only thing between outside air and the people breathing it.

## What it does

It traps dust, pollen and road grit before they reach the <Part id="hvac.evaporator" /> and the cabin. Keeping the evaporator fins clean matters as much as keeping the air clean, because a dirty core loses heat transfer and becomes a home for the microbial growth that causes air-conditioning odour.

Activated carbon versions add a second layer that adsorbs gases and odours — exhaust fumes, ozone — rather than particles. Higher-grade filters rated for fine particulate capture a meaningful share of PM2.5.

<Market region="Thailand">
This is a more consequential part here than in most markets. Seasonal PM2.5 episodes in Bangkok and the north regularly push air quality well past healthy limits, and a carbon or PM2.5-rated element is a genuine health measure rather than a comfort upgrade. Dust and humidity also mean the element loads faster than the book interval assumes — inspect at half interval.
</Market>

## How it fails

By clogging. The symptoms are weak airflow at all fan speeds, a musty smell, and a windscreen that demists slowly.

## How it is serviced

Replaced on interval, more often in dusty or high-pollution conditions. One of the easiest jobs on the car.
