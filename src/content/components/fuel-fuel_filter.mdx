A pleated medium between the tank and the injectors, sized to trap particles far smaller than anything you could see.

## What it does

Injector nozzles have holes measured in microns and clearances finer still, at pressures that turn any hard particle into a cutting tool. The filter is what stands between road-tanker sediment and those clearances.

Diesel filters do a second job that petrol filters do not: water separation. Diesel holds water in suspension, and water in a high-pressure pump causes rapid corrosion and seizure. Diesel filters therefore include a coalescing stage and a drain at the bottom.

<Market region="Thailand">
Biodiesel matters here. Thailand standardised on B7 in 2024 with B20 available for trucks and many pickups. Biodiesel is a mild solvent, so switching a vehicle that has run on low blends to B20 loosens old deposits and can block a filter quickly. Manufacturers advise shortened filter and oil intervals on continuous B20, and biodiesel-compatible lines and filters. Biodiesel is also more hygroscopic than mineral diesel, so draining the water trap matters more.
</Market>

## How it fails

By blocking, which starves the engine under load first — the car will idle but lose power on a hill.

## How it is serviced

Replaced on interval, more often on high biodiesel blends or dusty supply. Diesel water traps are drained at every service.
