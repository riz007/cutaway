The assembly the wheel bolts to, carrying the vehicle's weight while letting the wheel spin freely.

## What it does

It handles three loads at once. Radial load is the vehicle's weight pressing down. Axial load is the sideways thrust during cornering, which can exceed the vertical load. And it must resist the overturning moment the tyre applies through its contact patch.

Modern hubs use a sealed double-row angular contact bearing, pre-loaded at manufacture and never adjusted. Angular contact geometry is what handles the cornering thrust that a plain radial bearing could not.

The unit usually also carries the encoder ring for the <Part id="braking.wheel_speed_sensor" />, often as a magnetic strip moulded into the seal itself — which means a hub replacement can quietly resolve an ABS fault, and a careless hub installation can create one.

## How it fails

Seal failure letting water in, then corrosion and pitting. The classic symptom is a rumble or drone that changes with speed and often changes character when the car is steered, because cornering shifts load between the bearings.

## How it is serviced

Not adjustable or greasable. Replaced as a sealed unit.
