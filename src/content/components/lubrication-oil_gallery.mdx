Straight passages drilled through the block and head, with the ends plugged. Together they form the distribution network for pressurised oil.

## What it does

The main gallery runs the length of the block and feeds every main bearing. From each main bearing, a drilling continues through the <Part id="powertrain.crankshaft" /> web to the adjacent big-end journal, so the connecting rods are fed through the crankshaft itself rather than by any external pipe.

A separate feed climbs to the head for the camshaft journals and the <Part id="powertrain.vvt_actuator" />, usually through a restrictor, because the head needs far less flow than the crank and would otherwise steal pressure from it.

Gallery sizing is a balance: too small and pressure drops along the length, so the last bearing is starved; too large and the pump takes longer to build pressure at start-up.

<Note>
Most engine wear happens in the first seconds after a cold start, before oil has reached the far end of the galleries. This is why an anti-drainback valve in the filter and prompt oil pressure matter so much.
</Note>

## How it fails

Sludge blockage from neglected oil, and gallery plugs that work loose and cause a sudden total loss of pressure.

## How it is serviced

Only by keeping the oil clean.
