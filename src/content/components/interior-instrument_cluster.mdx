A modern cluster measures almost nothing itself. It is a display for data other modules have already produced.

## What it does

Road speed comes from the <Part id="braking.wheel_speed_sensor" /> signals via the <Part id="braking.abs_modulator" />. Engine speed and coolant temperature come from the <Part id="electrical.ecu_engine" />. Fuel level is one of the few things it may still read directly. All of it arrives over the <Part id="electrical.can_bus" />.

Warning lamps follow a colour convention worth knowing. Red means stop as soon as it is safe. Amber means a fault that needs attention but is not immediately dangerous. Green and blue are simply status.

<Note>
The coolant temperature gauge in most cars is deliberately damped. It sits at the middle of its range across a wide band of actual temperatures, so a driver does not worry about normal variation. The consequence is that by the time the needle visibly moves, the situation is already serious.
</Note>

The oil pressure lamp is the one that is not damped. It is a switch, not a gauge, and it means the <Part id="lubrication.oil_pump" /> is no longer maintaining the film the bearings ride on. Continuing to drive destroys the engine in under a minute.

## How it fails

Backlight and stepper motor faults on older units, and software or bus faults on newer ones.

## How it is serviced

Not a service item. Replacement units generally require mileage and immobiliser data to be transferred by a dealer tool.
