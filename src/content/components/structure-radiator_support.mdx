The frame across the front of the engine bay carrying the cooling pack, the headlights, the bonnet latch and often the bumper beam.

## What it does

It holds the <Part id="cooling.radiator" />, <Part id="hvac.condenser" /> and <Part id="cooling.cooling_fan" /> square to the airflow, and it sets the position of everything on the front of the car. Panel gaps around the bonnet and headlights are decided here, which is why a support that is out by a few millimetres shows up as visible misalignment.

It also completes the front structure by tying the two rails together at their forward ends, adding torsional stiffness to what would otherwise be two cantilevered beams.

Many modern supports are a plastic and steel composite: a moulded polymer carrier with steel inserts moulded in, which saves weight and lets complex mounting features be formed in one piece.

Some are bolt-on rather than welded, specifically to make front-end collision repair cheaper.

## How it fails

Impact damage, corrosion at the lower cross-member where road spray collects, and cracked plastic carriers.

## How it is serviced

Not serviced. Its squareness is checked whenever front-end panel gaps are wrong.
