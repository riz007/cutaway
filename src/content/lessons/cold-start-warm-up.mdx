title: Cold start to operating temperature
system: cooling
review: draft
steps:
  - title: Start cold
    body: The engine and coolant are at ambient. Press play and open the throttle a little. Watch the coolant temperature readout climb quickly, because only a small volume of coolant is circulating.
    system: cooling
    focus: cooling.water_pump
  - title: The thermostat is shut
    body: Coolant is going round the bypass loop, not the radiator. The radiator hoses stay cold. This is deliberate - a cold engine wears faster, burns more fuel and pollutes more, so it is warmed as quickly as possible.
    system: cooling
    focus: cooling.thermostat
  - title: The thermostat cracks open
    body: Around 82 degrees the wax pellet melts and the valve begins to open. Watch the rate of temperature rise flatten. Flow starts diverting through the radiator loop.
    system: cooling
    focus: cooling.radiator
  - title: The fan cuts in
    body: If temperature keeps climbing - in traffic, or with the air conditioning on - the fan starts pulling air through the core. Turn on the A/C and watch the fan duty change.
    system: cooling
    focus: cooling.cooling_fan
  - title: Where the cabin heat comes from
    body: The heater core taps the same loop. Switch on cabin heat and watch it pull a little energy out of the coolant. This is why weak cabin heat is often the first symptom of a thermostat stuck open.
    system: cooling
    focus: cooling.heater_core
---

A five-step tour of what actually happens between a cold start and a stable operating temperature, and why the thermostat is the component that decides all of it.
