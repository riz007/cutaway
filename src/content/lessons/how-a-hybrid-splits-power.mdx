steps:
  - title: No gearbox, no clutch
    body: A series-parallel hybrid has no gear ratios to shift and no clutch to slip. Everything is done by one planetary gearset and two electric machines.
    system: powertrain
    focus: powertrain.planetary_gearset
  - title: Three shafts, two degrees of freedom
    body: The engine, MG1 and MG2 are mechanically tied. Fix any two speeds and the third follows. So the controller can choose the engine speed it wants regardless of road speed.
    system: powertrain
    focus: powertrain.mg1
  - title: Driving on electricity alone
    body: At light load and low speed the engine stops entirely and MG2 drives the wheels from the battery. Set a small throttle and watch the hybrid mode readout.
    system: powertrain
    focus: powertrain.mg2
  - title: Recovering energy
    body: Lift off or brake and MG2 becomes a generator. Watch the pack power go negative and the regen share rise. Friction brakes only make up the difference.
    system: braking
    focus: braking.regen_blending
  - title: The pack
    body: All of that energy passes through the high-voltage battery, which is small on a full hybrid - about a kilowatt hour - because it is a buffer, not a fuel tank.
    system: powertrain
    focus: powertrain.hv_battery
