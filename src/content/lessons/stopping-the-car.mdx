steps:
  - title: The pedal
    body: The pedal is a lever. It multiplies your foot force by roughly four before anything hydraulic happens. Move the brake slider and watch the chain begin.
    system: braking
    focus: braking.pedal
  - title: The booster
    body: Atmospheric pressure acting on a large diaphragm multiplies the force again. Without it, a hard stop would take a leg press.
    system: braking
    focus: braking.booster
  - title: Force becomes pressure
    body: In the master cylinder, force divided by piston area becomes pressure - and pressure appears everywhere in the circuit at once. Watch the line pressure readout.
    system: braking
    focus: braking.master_cylinder
  - title: The modulator can override you
    body: Between the master cylinder and the wheels sits the ABS and stability control modulator. It can lower, hold or raise pressure at each corner independently, many times a second.
    system: braking
    focus: braking.abs_modulator
  - title: Clamp, torque, heat
    body: At the caliper, pressure becomes clamp force, clamp force becomes friction torque, and all the vehicle energy becomes heat in the rotor. Brake repeatedly and watch the rotor temperature climb faster than it can shed.
    system: braking
    focus: braking.rotor_front
---

Follow one input - your foot - all the way through to heat leaving a cast-iron disc.
